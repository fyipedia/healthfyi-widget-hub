⚠ Medical Disclaimer

Health FYI widgets are for educational reference only. They do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional (physician, pharmacist, registered dietitian) before:

  • Taking any medication or changing dosage
  • Combining medications (drug interactions)
  • Identifying an unknown pill — verify with a licensed pharmacist
  • Making dietary changes for a medical condition
  • Diagnosing symptoms based on anatomical reference

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call your local emergency services immediately. In the US, call 911. In Korea, 119.

Data sources (FDA NDC, USDA FoodData Central, DrugBank, RxNorm, Terminologia Anatomica) are authoritative but data may be incomplete or outdated. Treatments and contraindications vary by jurisdiction, patient history, and individual factors.

FAQ

What data sources does Health FYI use?

AnatomyFYI uses Terminologia Anatomica (FCAT/FIPAT). PillFYI uses FDA NDC + DailyMed. DrugFYI sources DrugBank + RxNorm. NutriFYI uses USDA FoodData Central.

Is this medical advice?

NO. Educational reference only. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional. See full disclaimer.

How accurate is pill identification?

PillFYI matches imprint code + color + shape against FDA NDC. Confidence-scored. ALWAYS verify with pharmacist before consuming any pill.

How does DrugFYI handle drug interactions?

References DrugBank: pharmacokinetic + pharmacodynamic interactions, CYP450 enzymes, contraindications. Severity Major/Moderate/Minor. Always confirm with prescribing physician.

Are nutrition values per 100g or per serving?

NutriFYI per USDA standard: per 100g for most foods, with per-typical-serving where defined. Macros, vitamins A-K, minerals, glycemic index.
